Delete Account

Second Wind allows users to delete their account from within the app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Second Wind app.
  2. Go to your profile or settings area.
  3. Select the account deletion option.
  4. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When your account is deleted, your saved dishes, favorites, preferences, and personal account data will be deleted within a reasonable period, unless retention is required by law, fraud prevention, security, or legitimate operational requirements.

Need Help?

If you cannot access the app or need assistance deleting your account, contact: support@struthersstudio.com
