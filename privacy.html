Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

Our Philosophy

Your kitchen is your business. Second Wind was built to spark creativity with the ingredients you already have — not to pry into your life. We collect only what the app needs to work, and we treat it with respect.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as email address and authentication details
  • Ingredients you enter to generate dishes
  • Dishes you generate, save, or favorite
  • Preferences and app settings
  • Basic app usage information used to improve the experience
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information used to improve stability

How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To generate dishes from your ingredients
  • To save your favorites and preferences
  • To improve app reliability, security, and performance
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising.

AI Processing

When you generate a dish, the ingredients and categories you provide are securely processed by trusted AI providers in order to create your dish, presentation notes, and beverage pairing.

AI-generated dishes may be incomplete or inaccurate. Use your judgment in the kitchen — especially with food allergies, dietary restrictions, and safe cooking practices. AI suggestions are not professional culinary, nutritional, or medical advice.

Third-Party Services

The app may use trusted third-party services to operate and improve the product, including Firebase, OpenAI or other AI providers, Apple services, and RevenueCat for subscriptions.

Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect your information. No online system can guarantee absolute security, but we work to safeguard your data and reduce unnecessary collection.

Your Choices and Rights

You may be able to access, edit, or delete your content within the app. You may also delete your account from within the app or request assistance through support.

When your account is deleted, associated personal information will be deleted within a reasonable period unless retention is required by law, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate operational requirements.

Children's Privacy

Second Wind is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be communicated through the app or website.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to: support@struthersstudio.com
